LYRIC SCROLL - how to put it online (Netlify, free)

1. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder called "lyric-scroll".
2. On a computer or Chromebook, sign in at netlify.com (free account).
3. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the whole "lyric-scroll" folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link. In Site settings you can change the name,
   e.g. lyricscroll.netlify.app.
5. Open the link on your phone. Chrome: tap "Install app".
   iPhone: Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.

UPDATING LATER
Drag the updated folder onto your site's Deploys page in Netlify.
Installed apps pick up the new version the next time they open online.

WHAT'S IN THE FOLDER (keep all files together)
index.html            the app
manifest.webmanifest  name, colors, and icons for installing
sw.js                 lets the app open offline
icons/                app icons
