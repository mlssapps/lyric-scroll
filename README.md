# Lyric Scroll

Turn your lyrics into a scrolling lyric video, timed to your song by tapping.

**Open the app:** https://mlssapps.github.io/lyric-scroll/

Lyric Scroll runs right in your browser on phones, tablets, Chromebooks, and computers. Your songs, pictures, and lyrics stay on your device; nothing is uploaded.

## What it does

- **Tap-to-time:** play your song and tap as each line should come in
- **Fix one line:** move a single line earlier or later, or re-tap just that line
- **Timed breaks:** a line with just `-` clears the screen for intros and instrumentals
- **Title card** that fades in and out on its own
- **Backgrounds:** transparent, green screen, solid color, or your own picture, with zoom, framing, and a darker/brighter slider
- **Style:** 15 fonts, any color, size, outline, glow, and position
- **Export:** MP4 video with or without the song, transparent WebM, or PNG frames, in 16:9, 9:16, square, or 4K, as the whole song or just a clip
- **Projects:** save each song's lyrics, timing, and style and pick up where you left off

## Quick start

1. Paste your lyrics, one line per row.
2. Under **Timing**, choose your song and press **Start tap timing**. Tap each time a new line should come in, then once more at the end.
3. Pick a font, color, and background.
4. Under **Export**, choose a frame shape and press **Record video**.
5. Tap **Save**. If nothing happens, **press and hold Save and choose Download link**.

The full guide is in the app under **How to use Lyric Scroll**.

## Install it like an app

- **Android, tablets, Chromebooks:** open the link in Chrome and tap **Install app**.
- **iPhone and iPad:** open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Once installed, it opens full screen and works offline.
