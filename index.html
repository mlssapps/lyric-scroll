Lyric Scroll

Version 17

Paste your lyrics, time them to your song, style them, and export a video of the lines rolling up one at a time.

How to use Lyric Scroll

Quick start

  1. Paste your lyrics, one line per row.
  2. Under Timing, choose your song, press Start tap timing, and tap the big button each time a new line should come in. Tap once more when the last line should leave.
  3. Press Play to check it. Pick a font, color, size, and position.
  4. Under Background, pick a background. Under Export, choose a frame shape and press Record video. Keep the screen open until it finishes.
  5. Tap the Save button that appears. If nothing happens, press and hold it and choose Download link.

Saving your files

When a video, PNG zip, or project file is ready, a Save [file name] button appears right below the button you pressed, showing the file size. Tap it to save the file to your Downloads folder.

If tapping Save does nothing (common when the app is opened from your phone's Downloads), press and hold the Save button and choose Download link. On iPhone and iPad, Save opens the share menu instead: choose Save Video or Save to Files.

Android may add (1) to a file name if one with the same name is already in Downloads. If a song file won't open later, rename it so it ends in .mp3 or .m4a again.

Timing

Tapped to the song lets every line land on the beat. Even spacing gives every line the same number of seconds and doesn't need a song.

To fix one line, pick it under Fix one line. Earlier and Later move it a tenth of a second. Re-tap from here replays a few seconds before that line and lets you re-tap from that point on, keeping everything above it.

If every line feels a little late (normal reaction time), slide Shift all lines to the left.

You can edit lyrics after tapping and keep your timing. Deleted lines are skipped, a reworded line keeps its time, and a new line is placed between its neighbors (marked ≈ in Fix one line). Pick it and press Tap this line to set just that one; every other line stays put.

Title and breaks

Type a title in Title line. It fades in and out over the background on its own. Set when it fades in and out (or leave the end empty and it fades out as the first lyric arrives).

To clear the screen mid-song, like during an instrumental, add a line with just - where the break goes and tap it in like a lyric. Every lyric fades away and only the background shows until the next line. Clear screen before the first lyric and after the last (in Timing) does the same at the start and end automatically.

Sections

Tap any section name to fold it up, so the preview stays close while you adjust settings. The app remembers which sections you keep closed.

Projects

New project clears everything back to the starting example so you can begin a new song (save the current one first). Give the song a title and press Save project to keep a small file with its lyrics, timing, and style. Open project brings it all back, so you can work on several songs or re-export one later in another frame shape. Choose the song and picture again after opening.

Backgrounds

Picture has Fill the frame (crops to fill) or Show whole picture (the full photo with black around it), plus a Darker ↔ Brighter slider: left for moody, right to wash out detail.

Use Zoom, Left ↔ Right, and Up ↔ Down to choose which part of the picture shows, like keeping the moon in frame when a wide photo is cropped for 9:16. Re-center picture resets them.

Transparent is for editors that support see-through video. Green screen is for phone editors like InShot and CapCut: add the video as a PiP or overlay layer and use Chroma Key to remove the green. Solid color and Picture make a finished lyric video you can post as-is.

Adding the song

Include the song in the video (in Export) puts the song chosen in Timing right into the video, matched to any clip you set. It works with picture and solid-color backgrounds, and you can turn it off anytime. If your phone can't add audio, the app says so and saves the video without sound.

Without it, the song isn't included in the video file. Add it in your editor or social app, starting at 0:00 so the lyrics stay in sync. Social apps often start a song at a highlight, so drag it back to the beginning.

To post a short clip, check Export only part of the song and set a start and end. Start the song at the same time in your app.

Install as an app

Android, tablets, Chromebooks: tap Install app at the top, or Chrome's menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). iPhone and iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Once installed, it opens full screen and works offline after the first visit.

Good to know

  • With a picture or solid background, the video is built frame by frame, usually faster than the song plays. Transparent videos still record in real time. Either way, keep the app open and the screen on until it finishes.
  • Transparent video works best in Chrome on a computer or Chromebook. iPhones save MP4, which can't be transparent, so use Green screen there.
  • PNG frames keep transparency anywhere and import into desktop editors as an image sequence.
  • Your lyrics, style, and timing are saved on this device. Songs and pictures aren't, so choose them again next time.
  • If the Choose song or Choose picture buttons don't open anything, open this page in Chrome or Safari instead of inside another app.
  • Remember to press Save project before you close the app or switch to a newer version, then use Open project in the new one.
